# wild_tree candidates

The brief is "wild_tree" in [the fifth wave](../briefs.md). One agent built three candidates from the same brief and style lock, each with its own construction strategy, and verified them together (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`). Each is one mesh per material: `timber` bark and two `foliage` greens.

- `wild_tree_a`, primitives: a faceted, twisted, flared cylinder trunk, buttresses of half-cones, tapered limbs, ten lumpy icosahedron clumps with lobes on top, vines as cylinder chains. Verifier: `ok wild_tree_a 5304 tris 3 meshes 13.558x15.785x13.278m`.
- `wild_tree_b` (picked), profiles: a lofted five-lobed trunk whose flutes twist about 60 degrees up to 8.8 m, buttresses extruded from a concave side profile, limbs, branches and vines as swept tapering tubes, eleven lumpy lathed cushions. Verifier: `ok wild_tree_b 7770 tris 3 meshes 14.148x15.914x13.302m`.
- `wild_tree_c`, a second reading: six swept strands that braid up to 7 m, then part in pairs into three twin limbs, each ending in a crown pad, with lofted buttresses that curl with the twist. Verifier: `ok wild_tree_c 7648 tris 3 meshes 13.014x15.937x13.328m`.

Picked B by looking at the three in the game, through the frozen reach's great ring onto the forest (`scratch/ingame_a.png`, `ingame_b.png`, `ingame_c.png`), and from the ground (`scratch/ground_views.png`). Through the door the trunks are what the eye meets at its own height: B's fluted, twisting trunks and buttresses read as old wood. A's crowns read as cotton balls and C's tiered pads as umbrellas, not a wild canopy.

Weaknesses, from the agent's report: the pillow-like cushions are softer than the style lock's chunky masses, and colouring by height makes the crown read in two layers; the buttress planks are straight in plan; the vine leaves are thin edge-on; it is the heaviest of the three.
