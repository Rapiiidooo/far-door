# field_scroll candidates

The brief is "field_scroll" in [the sixth wave](../briefs.md). One agent built three candidates from the same brief and style lock, each with its own construction strategy, and verified them together (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`). Parchment, ink and cord are all named `fabric` (left unnamed, the dark ink would take the roof-tile texture); the map is raised ink: a river, three peaks, a dashed trail, a circle and a cross.

- `field_scroll_a`, primitives: a cylinder roll with a spiral of dark half-rings on its ends, a thin-box sheet with an eight-box curl, box strokes, a flattened torus and crossed boxes for the ink, a cord of two open torus wraps with a knot and hanging ends. Verifier: `ok field_scroll_a 1408 tris 71 meshes 0.302x0.1x0.488m`.
- `field_scroll_b` (picked), profiles: one hand-swept profile with real thickness, a four-turn spiral running out into the flat sheet and its curl, dark fans inside the ends, the ink swept as raised beads with a lathed ring and an extruded cross, a swept-tube cord with a lathed knot. Verifier: `ok field_scroll_b 1384 tris 13 meshes 0.3x0.105x0.492m`.
- `field_scroll_c`, a second reading: the roll's inner turns slid along its axis, a sheet with low waves and a skewed curl, the knot on top with its frayed ends lying on the map. Verifier: `ok field_scroll_c 1446 tris 12 meshes 0.522x0.106x0.35m`.

Picked B in the game, lying on the court floor by the urns where the first relic waits ([the three at a metre](ingame_close.png)). A and B read the same from a metre, map and cord included; B is the same scroll built as one spiral and a swept cord, where A's cord kinks at its joints. C's wide landscape map is the most worn, but its cord covers the middle of the map. The game tints it towards old parchment: in the court's sun the bone limestone reads white.

Weaknesses, from the agent's report: the tidiest and least worn of the three, with a flat sheet and a regular curl; the outer turn is slightly faceted up close; from the front the curl hides the cross; the rope-coloured cord fades first, at about 5 m. The first relic, the first papyrus, uses it, and so does Mira's lost page on the isles.
