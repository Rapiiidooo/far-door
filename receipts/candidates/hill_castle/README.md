# hill_castle candidates

One agent built three candidates from the same brief and style lock, each with its own construction strategy, and verified them together (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`). Each exposes `userData.parts.windows`, one mesh of lantern amber; stone is `stone`, roofs `tile`, spires `metal`; each is 42 m tall and seven meshes.

- `hill_castle_a`, primitives: five towers stacked from cylinders with steep cones on an irregular ring, walls of boxes between them, a gatehouse with a voussoir arch and a gabled hall in the court. Verifier: `ok hill_castle_a 5427 tris 7 meshes 36x42x24.3m`.
- `hill_castle_b`, profiles: lathed towers with battered feet and flared roofs on a pentagon, walls swept along each line with extruded merlons, a gate extruded through the wall. Verifier: `ok hill_castle_b 7317 tris 7 meshes 36.506x42x25.84m`.
- `hill_castle_c` (picked), a second reading: a ridge castle whose five towers are staggered along the crest, each crowned with a gallery on corbels and a parapet, the gate a tall arch through a pale portal. Verifier: `ok hill_castle_c 5270 tris 7 meshes 36.354x42x19.1m`.

Picked C in the game, on its hill in the haze through the frozen reach's great ring at the finale's framing ([the three](ingame_finale.png)). Seen only from the front and far off, C shows all five towers across the skyline, where A and B bunch theirs; its shallow plan never shows. The forest draws it half a size larger (about 61 m), sets it on a hill with a level top, hazes its stone by hand instead of by the fog, which at that distance would swallow it, and leaves its windows lit.

Weaknesses, from the agent's report: shallow, so its courtyard is a strip; the corbels and merlons fade beyond about 100 m; the windows are flat panes that look like stickers at grazing angles; the stepped portal top may read as a gable.
