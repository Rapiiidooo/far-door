# mira_lantern candidates

The brief is "mira_lantern" in [the sixth wave](../briefs.md). One agent built three candidates from the same brief and style lock, each with its own construction strategy, and verified them together (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`). Each exposes `userData.parts.flame`, a mesh of its own in lantern amber, and `userData.light`, the flame's centre for a point light; bronze is `metal`, the panes are pale limestone at 0.55 opacity so the flame shows through.

- `mira_lantern_a` (picked), primitives: a stepped cylinder foot with a torus rim, box posts, rails and panes, a roof of two open frustums with twelve gores and a soot cone behind the vents, a bail of two legs and a half torus. Verifier: `ok mira_lantern_a 1888 tris 5 meshes 0.242x0.45x0.226m`.
- `mira_lantern_b`, profiles: a turned foot, lathed square frames, angle-iron posts, a turned roof pierced with two rows of square holes, a bail swept along a squared arch, a turned teardrop flame. Verifier: `ok mira_lantern_b 2292 tris 5 meshes 0.26x0.45x0.228m`.
- `mira_lantern_c`, a second reading: a taller drum and a body that flares towards the top, round corner rods, a steep cone with eight round holes, a bail bent square over the top with a timber grip. Verifier: `ok mira_lantern_c 2344 tris 6 meshes 0.265x0.45x0.242m`.

Picked A in the game, where the relic stands in the lee of the frozen reach's island at dusk with its flame lit and a point light at the flame ([the three at a metre](ingame_close.png)). All three read as a lantern; A's arched bail and slotted roof give the clearest outline and it is the lightest, B's broad roof catches the flame's glow as a hot spot, and C's square bail and timber grip read as a camping lantern rather than an explorer's.

Weaknesses, from the agent's report: the bail's straight legs and half circle look mechanical; the vents are gaps between thin gores; seen from the side the bail is edge-on and looks like a spike; from the exact diagonal a corner post hides the flame; nothing shows wear beyond wax drips and soot.
