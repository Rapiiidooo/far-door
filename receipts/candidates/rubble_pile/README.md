# rubble_pile candidates

The brief is "rubble_pile" in [the fifth wave](../briefs.md). One agent built three candidates from the same brief and style lock, each with its own construction strategy, and verified them together (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`). All four materials are named `stone`.

- `rubble_pile_a` (picked), primitives: each block a dressed head and two courses round a groove core, built from cylinders whose rings are moved onto the chamfered section and broken on an oblique plane; the sunlit block leans on the other's dressed end; a faceted drum with its bone band, snapped in a step; twelve chunks. Verifier: `ok rubble_pile_a 821 tris 41 meshes 2.388x0.907x1.961m`.
- `rubble_pile_b`, profiles: each block one extrusion of its grooved section, its far end pulled onto a break closed by a triangulated fracture; a lathed drum with a rough slanted snap; eleven bevelled chips. Verifier: `ok rubble_pile_b 1042 tris 20 meshes 2.357x0.938x1.955m`.
- `rubble_pile_c`, a second reading: every piece a convex solid cut by planes, the sunlit slab propped steeply, the drum end-on with its snap to the front. Verifier: `ok rubble_pile_c 712 tris 20 meshes 2.373x0.884x1.958m`.

Picked A on the sheet and in the agent's renders at 15 m in low sun through the game's loader (`scratch/z15_rubble_pile_a.png` and its siblings); the in-game frames in the court (`scratch/ingame_court_*.png`) hide the pile behind a boulder and could not separate them. A's leaning grooved block and its banded drum read at once as fallen masonry of the builders; B's fractures look crumpled, and C is the plainest.

Weaknesses, from the agent's report: the drum's bed end, with its bone band, reads close to a barrel lid; the chunks are regular solids and look a little toy-like; each break is one flat plane per course, so it looks sawn up close.
