# mira_scarf candidates

The brief is "mira_scarf" in [the sixth wave](../briefs.md). One agent built three candidates from the same brief and style lock, each with its own construction strategy, and verified them together (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`). The stone is `stone`, the scarf (vermilion and ochre) `fabric`, the frost `ground`; the scarf and the frost are double-sided.

- `mira_scarf_a`, primitives: a stone of stacked frustum layers, a scarf of 62 flattened cylinders, one per knit course, laid along a path found by casting rays onto the stone, two gently bent tails, knotted tassels. Verifier: `ok mira_scarf_a 2510 tris 119 meshes 1.61x0.292x0.572m`.
- `mira_scarf_b` (picked), profiles: a lathed boulder with angular ripples, the scarf one swept band whose thickness alternates ring by ring for the ribs, crossing the boulder diagonally and bending across its width to follow it, one soft fold in the back tail, lathed tassels. Verifier: `ok mira_scarf_b 2680 tris 15 meshes 1.357x0.326x0.982m`.
- `mira_scarf_c`, a second reading: the scarf folded in half over a slab, its loop hanging behind and its two fringed ends splayed in a V in front. Verifier: `ok mira_scarf_c 2569 tris 26 meshes 0.664x0.335x1.087m`.

Picked B in the game, on the snow of the plateau above the frozen pond where the relic waits ([near and from the pond's notch](ingame.png)). All three read as a red scarf from the notch; up close B's diagonal drape and trailing ends look like a scarf dropped in passing, A's is laid out symmetrically and C's folded V is compact. Taken, the scarf and its frost leave the bare stone behind.

Weaknesses, from the agent's report: the boulder is a fairly generic dome and its frost reads as a snow cap rather than a dusting; up close the frost flecks on the ribs look like white dashes; the diagonal gives the deepest footprint; the fold in the tail is hand-placed.
