# fallen_head candidates

The brief fixes 4.5 x 2.6 x 3 m; every expect file uses those sizes. All three rebuild the head of `guardian_colossus` (mask and slit, back channels, tier one with its groove and cap, the disc on tier two) and lay it on its right side, face to +Z, headdress to -X.

- A, primitives: crossed boxes with stepped corners, the mask as two tipped halves round a wedge crack, a cylinder-and-torus disc, dodecahedron rubble, resting on the floor in flattened sphere caps. `ok  fallen_head_a  2516 tris  51 meshes  4.65x2.753x3.22m`
- B, profiles: chamfered plan outlines extruded upward with the bevel growth subtracted, side silhouettes extruded across the width for the sheared neck and tier, the cracked mask as two extruded outlines, lathes for the disc and the drift. `ok  fallen_head_b  3526 tris  22 meshes  4.617x2.742x3.33m`
- C, hand-built lofts in the guardian's own sections, laid down and cut at the floor so it is really sunk, rough broken faces on the neck and tier two, a heightfield drift banked against the back and ends. `ok  fallen_head_c  3018 tris  22 meshes  4.846x2.577x3.184m`

Picked C. In the game's own light (rig at 15.2 h, surfaces on) all three read as the fallen guardian's head at 15 m, but C shares the standing guardian's exact sections, its V-cut crack and settled chin slab catch the raking sun, and it alone sits in the sand instead of on it. A reads as a cabinet with two doors; B's sheared ends look fluted and its lathed drifts read as smooth pillows.

Known weaknesses: the break on tier two still shows faint diagonal creases from the square rings; the drift is sand-coloured `ground`, a shade paler than the flagstone floor, so it reads as a small dune rather than blending into flagstones.
