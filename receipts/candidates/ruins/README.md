# The ruins wave

The briefs are the ruins of the ninth wave in [briefs.md](../briefs.md): the city under the sea that the forest's last door shows, now drowned and broken. An agent built one candidate per brief, with the same radii and placements as the pristine city of the [sea wave](../sea/README.md), and verified them together until all six were clean (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`):

- `ruin_temple_a`: 4,156 triangles, 49.6 x 35.2 x 50.9 m. The dome's gash is off centre so it does not read as a clamshell; the crystal glows in the breached sanctum and the lantern lies on the steps. Lower than the brief's 40 to 45 m because the lantern has fallen and nothing rises above the dome.
- `ruin_rings_a`: 6,616 triangles, 196 x 5.8 x 194 m. About seventy arcs of wall of varying height with gaps and fallen blocks, a breach along +Z, causeways with broken spans and lone piers, and light surviving along about half of the arcs.
- `ruin_house_a`: 712 triangles, 4.51 x 4.53 x 5.2 m.
- `ruin_tower_a`: 460 triangles, 6.0 x 16.4 x 6.0 m, with its fallen drums and shard.
- `ruin_column_a`: 352 triangles, 3.45 x 5.56 x 2.26 m, with its two drums.
- `ruin_arch_a`: 728 triangles, 10.6 x 9.3 x 4.0 m.

Each is copied to `game/assets/` under its brief's name. The game sets the houses and towers on the sand between the rings and outside them rather than on the walls, whose tops are broken, and adds pieces of the sunken court (two guardian colossi leaning at the avenue's mouth, fallen heads, broken columns, rubble and amphorae), washed towards the water's colour. The weaknesses the agent saw: the crack detail within a block is faint up close, the column's fluting shallow at a distance, and the sheet's grey studio light is not the game's blue fog.
