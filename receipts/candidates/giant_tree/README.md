# giant_tree candidates

The brief is "giant_tree" in [the sixth wave](../briefs.md). One agent built three candidates from the same brief and style lock, each with its own construction strategy, and verified them together (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`). Each exposes `userData.parts.glow`, a mesh of twelve pods with its own spore lime emissive; bark is `timber`, moss and fern are `foliage`; each loads through the game's loader as four meshes.

- `giant_tree_c` (picked), a second reading: each of the eight roots one strand swept from a buttress plank into a rope, braided about 100 degrees up a notched core and parting in pairs into four limbs, each with its own heap of clumps. Verifier: `ok giant_tree_c 11624 tris 4 meshes 44.082x60.423x44.495m`.

Picked C in the game, through the frozen reach's great ring at the finale's framing ([the three](ingame_finale.png), [the forest with the pick](ingame_forest.png)) and on the agent's renders in forest light at 150 m (`scratch/look_final.png`). At that distance A and B read as the forest's own tree grown large, which the brief warns against; C's braided trunk reads at once as something older and stranger. The forest sets it a little into the ground so no root tip floats, and turns its hollow towards the door.

Weaknesses, from the agent's report: the heaviest of the three, near the 12,000 budget; its four heaps still read mostly as one dome from above; face-based moss has jagged edges; the roots look thin and spiky in three-quarter view; the twist may read as rope rather than one massive trunk; its hollow is narrower than B's.
