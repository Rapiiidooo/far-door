# The sea creatures, second reading

The briefs are the ninth wave's `reef_fish` and `manta_ray` in [briefs.md](../briefs.md), to replace the eighth wave's plain ones. An agent built two candidates of each and verified them together, four of four clean (`_verify/sheet.png`, `--size=560`):

- `reef_fish_a` (picked), 407 triangles, 0.21 x 0.75 x 1 m: a body swept from a lathe profile, a forked tail with a dark trailing edge, tall swept dorsal and anal fins, pectoral fins, ringed eyes and three dark bands.
- `reef_fish_b`, 421 triangles: the same fins and bands on a sphere reshaped by moving its vertices; more torpedo-like, less elegant.
- `manta_ray_a`, 1,007 triangles: one extruded planform with a domed hub; it reads more as a shield than as a ray.
- `manta_ray_b` (picked), 830 triangles, 1 x 0.09 x 1 m: a domed hub and two flat swept wing panels with pale under-wings, cephalic fins, eyes and a whip tail; the tips and the belly show from every side.

Picked by eye on the sheet and then in the game, where the shoals tint the fish's pale body gold, silver, blue or coral and leave the bands and eyes dark. Every material but the body is kept dark enough that the game's tint never takes it. Weaknesses: the mantas' cephalic fins read as antennae head-on, and the fish's eye ring is faint at its size.
